Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. This policy explains what data Chicken Joe collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account data: if you register, we store your email address, a username and a securely hashed password. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • Content you create: surf reports, votes and beaches you add are stored and shown publicly alongside your username.
  • Local storage: we use your browser's local storage to remember your login session and your list of favourite beaches. This stays on your device.
  • Location: if you grant permission, we use your device location only to show nearby beaches. It is processed in your browser and not stored on our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and how the app is used, in aggregate. This helps us improve the product. Analytics may set cookies and collect anonymised usage data such as pages viewed and approximate region.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo — provides marine and weather forecast data.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — powers beach search and map previews.
  • Google Analytics & AdSense — analytics and advertising.

Your choices

You can clear your local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. If you'd like your account and associated reports deleted, contact us and we'll remove them.

Children

Chicken Joe is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.
